## Why / 為什麼

Using AI coding agents for real work, the same problems keep coming back:

- The AI says "done", but nobody verified it.
- Files from different projects get mixed up.
- Every new session has to relearn what went wrong last time.

用 AI 做事，常卡在三件事：做完了對不對沒人確認、專案一多資料就混、換個 AI 又要從頭教。

## What it does / 它做什麼

Every job goes through seven steps:

1. Request — say what you want
2. Criteria — write how "done" will be checked
3. Dispatch — assign who makes and who checks
4. Make — the AI does the work
5. Independent check — a **separate conversation** checks the result against the criteria
6. Evidence — collect the proof
7. Decision — pass, or send it back to the step that went wrong

每件工作走七步：說要求、定標準、派工、製作、另開對話檢查、收證、判定。做的和查的分開，不合格就退回出錯的那一步。

- **Making and checking are kept apart.** The checker must really be a different conversation — verified from the AI's own session records, not from what the AI says about itself.
- **Files stay with their own project.** Data is labeled by project, so work doesn't leak across projects.
- **Lessons are passed on.** Recorded failures are attached to later jobs automatically, so the next AI doesn't repeat them.

It does not make the AI stop making mistakes. It makes the mistakes get caught and fixed.
它不保證 AI 不犯錯，是讓錯誤被發現、被修正。
